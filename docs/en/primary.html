Singapore method

Understanding first, then speed

The Singapore method, used by schools around the world, teaches children to understand an idea before they memorise rules. Pupils move through three steps:

  1. C

    Concrete

    Children touch and move real objects: blocks, counters, cards.

  2. P

    Pictorial

    They draw what they did, using models such as the famous “bar model”.

  3. A

    Abstract

    Only then do they write numbers and symbols, now with real understanding.

In the timetable

Robotics, chess and komuz are lessons here

Unlike other private schools, these subjects are part of the weekly timetable for every primary pupil, alongside further clubs after lessons.

  • Robotics

    Building and programming robots: engineering thinking from the first grades.

  • Chess

    Logic, focus and planning, and learning to win and lose with dignity.

  • Komuz

    Playing the national instrument: rhythm, memory and love for Kyrgyz culture.

  • Extra clubs

    Art, sport, English speaking club and more, so every child finds their thing.

English

More English, from Grade 1

Our primary pupils have more English lessons than in most schools. The aim is practice: children listen, speak, sing, read and play in English every week, with our English teachers and native English-speaking teachers.

  • More English hours than in a typical school
  • Native English-speaking teachers
  • Speaking, songs, games and reading
  • Ready for the Cambridge programme in Grade 7
