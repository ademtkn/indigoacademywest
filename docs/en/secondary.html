Cambridge pathway

Step by step to Grade 12

The Cambridge programme builds knowledge and skills year after year, from the first secondary grades to graduation.

  1. Grades 7–8

    Foundations

    Pupils adapt to the Cambridge programme and to learning across subjects in English.

    • Cambridge Lower Secondary subjects
    • Study and research skills
    • Academic English

  2. Grades 9–10

    Deeper study

    Subjects become more demanding and pupils start to choose their focus.

    • Mathematics, sciences and English in depth
    • Projects and presentations
    • Olympiads and competitions

  3. Grades 11–12

    Ready for university

    Focused preparation for international examinations and university admission.

    • International examinations
    • IELTS and university preparation
    • Career and university guidance

The exact sequence of Cambridge stages and examinations is confirmed with families at admission.

One-to-one attention

Every pupil has a plan

Teenagers need both challenge and support. Our teachers know each pupil well: their strengths, their gaps and their goals.

Progress is reviewed regularly, extra support is offered where it is needed, and pupils who are ready for more are given more. Parents always know how their child is doing.
