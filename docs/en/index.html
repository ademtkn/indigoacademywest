Welcome

Every child is known, heard and challenged

At Indigo Academy West, girls and boys learn side by side in small classes where the teacher has time for every pupil. We believe children learn best when they feel safe, are curious, and are asked to think rather than just to remember.

Our primary school combines the Russian-language programme with the Singapore method of teaching. From Grade 7 our pupils move to the Cambridge programme, and all along the way English is part of every day.

Why Indigo West

What makes our school different

More English

More English lessons than in most schools, with native-speaking teachers on our staff. Children practise English every day, not only in class.

Singapore method

Primary teachers use the Singapore approach: from objects to pictures to abstract ideas, so pupils truly understand, not just memorise.

More than lessons

Robotics, chess, komuz and other extra subjects are part of the timetable, not an optional extra after school.

One-to-one attention

Like every Indigo Academy school, we follow each pupil individually: their pace, their strengths and what they need next.

Our school

Two schools, one path from Grade 1 to Grade 12

A single campus for the whole school journey. Pupils move from primary to high school without changing school, friends or values.

In the timetable

Subjects that set us apart

At other schools these are clubs. At Indigo Academy West they are lessons, taught every week by specialist teachers.

  • Robotics

    Children build, program and test their own robots and learn engineering thinking.

  • Chess

    Concentration, patience and logic, developed one move at a time.

  • Komuz

    The national instrument of Kyrgyzstan: music, memory and respect for our culture.

  • Extra English

    More English hours each week, with native-speaking teachers.

English every day

English that children actually speak

Our goal is simple: children should use English, not only study it. That is why our pupils have more English lessons than in most schools, and why native English-speaking teachers work on our staff.

  • More English lessons every week
  • Native English-speaking teachers
  • Speaking practice in small groups
