Admissions

We are glad that you are considering Indigo Academy West for your child. Our admissions team will guide you through every step.

Step by step

How to join us

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Call or message us on WhatsApp to ask about places in your child’s grade.

  2. 2

    Visit the school

    Tour the building, see lessons and meet the admissions team.

  3. 3

    Meet and assess

    A friendly conversation for future first graders. From Grade 2, a short assessment in mathematics and English.

  4. 4

    Submit documents

    Bring the documents from the list below to the school office.

  5. 5

    Welcome to Indigo West

    Sign the agreement, receive the uniform information and get ready for the first day.

Documents

What to prepare

  • Copy of the birth certificate
  • Copies of both parents’ passports
  • Medical record (form 026/u) and vaccination card
  • Personal file and report card from the previous school (for transfers)
  • 3×4 cm photographs, 4 pieces
  • Application form completed at the school office

The final list may change. Please confirm it with the school office before your visit.

Tuition fees

Tuition is set for each academic year. Please contact the admissions office for current fees and payment options.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which grades do you teach?

We teach Grades 1–12. Primary school is Grades 1–6; middle and high school, with the Cambridge programme, is Grades 7–12.

Is the school for boys and girls?

Yes. Indigo Academy West is a co-educational school: boys and girls study together.

In which language are primary classes taught?

Primary classes follow the Russian-language programme, with extra English lessons every week and Kyrgyz language lessons.

What is the Singapore method?

A way of teaching, especially mathematics, in which children first work with objects, then with pictures and models, and only then with numbers and symbols. It builds real understanding.

Are robotics, chess and komuz paid extras?

No, at Indigo Academy West robotics, chess and komuz are part of the timetable. Please ask the office about any additional clubs.

Do you have native English-speaking teachers?

Yes. Native English-speaking teachers are part of our teaching staff.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, and we recommend it. Call us to agree a convenient day and time for a personal tour.
