About Us

Indigo Academy West is a school of the Indigo Academy network in Bishkek. Boys and girls study here from Grade 1 to Grade 12.

Our story

An Indigo school for the west of Bishkek

Indigo Academy West belongs to Indigo Academy, a network of private schools in Bishkek known for its strong teaching and close attention to every child.

Families asked for a school that combines a solid academic base with modern methods, real English practice and a broader education. Indigo Academy West is our answer: a co-educational school for Grades 1–12, with the Singapore method in primary school and the Cambridge programme from Grade 7.

Our mission

To educate curious, well-mannered and confident young people who think independently, speak several languages and are ready for the world.

Our vision

To be the school families in Bishkek choose when they want a strong education, real English and international opportunities for their children.

Our values

Five words we live by

  1. Respectfor teachers, for classmates and for every person.
  2. Curiosityto ask questions and to look for answers.
  3. Responsibilityfor our learning, our words and our school.
  4. Kindnessshown in small everyday actions.
  5. Opennessto new languages, cultures and ideas.

Director’s welcome

Dear parents and guests

Thank you for your interest in Indigo Academy West. Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes, and we are honoured that you are considering us.

Our teachers know every child by name. They notice when a pupil is ready for a bigger challenge and when they need a little more support. We measure success not only in grades, but in how confidently our pupils think, speak and work with others, in their own language and in English.

I warmly invite you to visit us, to see our lessons and to talk with our team.

Director, Indigo Academy West
